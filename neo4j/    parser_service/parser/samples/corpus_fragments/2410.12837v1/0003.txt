Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) is an emerging hybrid architecture designed to address thelimitations of pure generative models. RAG integrates two key components: (i) a retrieval mechanism,which retrieves relevant documents or information from an external knowledge source, and (ii) ageneration module, which processesthisinformationtogeneratehuman-liketext (Lewiset al. 2020). Thiscombination allows RAG models to not only generate fluent text but also ground their outputs inreal-world, up-to-datedata. The retrieval module in RAG typically leverages dense vector representations to identify relevantdocuments from large datasets, such as Wikipedia or proprietary databases.
